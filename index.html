03

The foundation: the right person, by design

Nothing above works if the data underneath is wrong. Where this started: a rep opening a brief about a different person with the same name. Both corrections below are rules now.

Before · real output

"Group Chief Revenue Officer & Head of Partnerships" — a title belonging to someone else with the same name. The whole brief described the wrong career.

Tracing each wrong fact later took a full audit of a recorded call.

Now

RULEThe CRM fields drive every search: name, company and title together. A bare name never triggers research.

Why it holds: wrong-person is designed out at the input, not caught at the output.

Before · real state

"Only your workspace owner or manager can connect to data sources." A rep accepting an invite inherited one shared connection and ten thousand leads that were not theirs.

No step in the invite flow asked the rep to connect their own seat.

Now · shipped

SHIPPEDEvery rep connects their own CRM seat during invite setup. The picker leads with the team's CRM. Only their leads come in.

Why it holds: one seat, one rep, their own leads.

04

What was removed, on purpose

Removing was the feature. Each line below shipped to real buyers. Each is gone, with the rule that killed it.

  • The ask

    How do you currently ensure sales teams is interpreting her strategy and operations…

    Built on the wrong person's data, with no reason to be the main question. Replaced by one meeting objective, per call, from the record.

  • Invented numbers

    Reduce the time spent on manual prep by 50% and increase your partnership close rates by 15%?

    No source. Same pitch for every buyer. Rule: no receipt, no sentence.

  • Headcount fiction

    A focused group of 10 to 15 sellers — proposed to a founder who works alone.

    Shipped word for word to two buyers. Rule: team size comes from her profile or stays an open question.

  • Narration

    When she answers what made the call worth taking, name it back once and hold it for the close.

    Stage directions, twenty-eight in a list. Replaced by lines the rep says, one at a time.

  • The sticker

    PROBLEM · CHALLENGER over a paragraph that could carry any label.

    The technique never changed the conversation's shape. Rule: method first, every block serves a step of it.

  • The build log

    POST /api/parse · 2.9 s · 12,285 bytes opened the document.

    Fourteen pages, the buyer three pages in. Replaced by one printable page.

  • Shared script

    18 lines identical between two buyers running different techniques. Nine full sentences, word for word.

    The generator filled a template and changed the nouns. Rule: repeated lines are counted defects. Target zero.

05

The standard

What we hold ourselves to · targets, not shipped
  • Quality scored by a judge that reads. Floor: 88 of 100 per buyer, every note with a quoted receipt.
  • Zero identical spoken lines between buyers, counted on every generation.
  • The coach tracks the same arc the rep prepared. On track means silence. A missed step means one prompt.
  • One printable page per buyer, from the same plan the coach runs.

The guess is gone. What replaced it proves itself, every generation, against numbers.
