02

The script, before → after

Left quotes are real, shipped to buyers. Right is the shape that replaces them.

Before · the opening, any buyer

"Soo, thanks for making the time. Rather than a generic presentation, I want to understand how you build revenue from zero, what happens when you're engaging buyers, and how you know a venture is truly progressing. Does that sound reasonable?"

One opening sentence, forty-seven words. From the live record.

The rep's first line, buried forty lines deep in a 7,670-character document.

After · the opening [SAMPLE]

"Before we begin, what made this conversation worth taking?"

Then wait. The buyer speaks first. Already in the record, line two.

Why it is better: the opening earns the buyer's reason, in their words, before any teaching.

Before · the numbers

"What if we could show you how to reduce the time spent on manual prep by 50% and increase your partnership close rates by 15%?"

Invented. Identical pitch for every buyer.

After · the numbers [SAMPLE]

RULEShe works alone. Any pilot must fit one person. Source: her own profile.

Why it is better: no receipt, no sentence. Numbers cite where they came from.

03

Enrichment, before → after

The brief is only as good as the person underneath it. Where this started: a rep opening a brief about a different person with the same name.

Before · real output

"Group Chief Revenue Officer & Head of Partnerships" — a title belonging to someone else with the same name. The whole brief described the wrong career.

Tracing each wrong fact later took a full audit of a recorded call.

Now

RULEThe CRM fields drive every search: name, company and title together. A bare name never triggers research.

Why it holds: wrong-person is designed out at the input, not caught at the output.

Before · real state

"Only your workspace owner or manager can connect to data sources." A rep accepting an invite inherited one shared connection and ten thousand leads that were not theirs.

No step in the invite flow asked the rep to connect their own seat.

Now · shipped

SHIPPEDEvery rep connects their own CRM seat during invite setup. The picker leads with the team's CRM. Only their leads come in.

Why it holds: one seat, one rep, their own leads.

04

What was removed, on purpose

Removing was the feature. Each line shipped to real buyers. Each is gone, with the rule that killed it.

  • The ask

    How do you currently ensure sales teams is interpreting her strategy and operations…

    Built on the wrong person's data, with no reason to be the main question. Replaced by one meeting objective, per call, from the record.

  • Headcount fiction

    A focused group of 10 to 15 sellers — proposed to a founder who works alone.

    Shipped word for word to two buyers. Rule: team size comes from her profile or stays an open question.

  • Narration

    [Context] When Soo describes her current GTM strategy, ask how she ensures consistency… — twenty-two moves in this shape, in the live record.

    Stage directions dressed as a script. Replaced by lines the rep says, one at a time, grouped under the stage they serve.

  • The sticker

    PROBLEM · CHALLENGER over a paragraph that could carry any label.

    The technique was a tag on generic text. Rule: method chosen first, by code, every block serves a step of it.

  • The build log

    POST /api/parse · 2.9 s · 12,285 bytes opened the document.

    Fourteen pages, the buyer three pages in. Replaced by one printable page.

  • Shared script

    18 lines identical between two buyers running different techniques. Nine full sentences, word for word.

    The generator filled a template and changed the nouns. Rule: repeated lines are counted defects. Target zero.

05

The standard

What the pre-call holds itself to · targets, not shipped
  • Blueprint quality scored by a judge that reads. Floor: 88 of 100 per buyer, every note with a quoted receipt.
  • Zero identical spoken lines between buyers, counted on every generation.
  • Every number, headcount and percentage carries a source, or the sentence is not written.
  • One printable page per buyer, ready in the ninety seconds before the call.

The guess is gone. What replaced it proves itself, every generation, against numbers.
