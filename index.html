Pre-call, rebuilt

The pre-call used to guess about the buyer. Now it runs the call.

Eight problems named in the review. Each one below shows the real thing that shipped, and what ships instead.

1Rep connects their own CRM seat→ 2The right person is found→ 3Personality read, as hypothesis→ 4The method shapes the script→ 5Coach speaks only on a missed step
01

It researched the wrong person

Search went by name alone. Same name, different person, wrong company, wrong title.

Before · real output

"Group Chief Revenue Officer & Head of Partnerships" — a title that belonged to someone else with the same name. The brief was about the wrong career entirely.

The fix that followed took a full audit of the call to trace where each wrong fact came from.

Now

BASEThe CRM fields drive the search: name, company and title together. A name alone never triggers enrichment.

Wrong-person is now a designed-out failure, not a monitored one.

02

The main question was invented

A generated "best question to ask" sat on every opportunity, built from nothing.

Before · real output

"How do you currently ensure sales teams is interpreting her strategy and operations…"

Asked about a role the buyer never had. Based on the wrong person's data.

No source, no reason for that question to exist, no reason for that question to be first.

Now

SHIPPEDThe generated question is gone. One meeting objective stays, written per call, sourced from the record.

Nothing appears on the card without a reason to be there.

03

It was a document about the call, not a script

The "script" narrated the call instead of giving words to say.

Before · real output

"When she answers what made the call worth taking, name it back once and hold it for the close."

Stage directions. Twenty-eight of them in a list. Nothing a rep could say out loud.

Now · [SAMPLE]

Ask: "What made this call worth taking for you?"

Then wait. Let the buyer speak first.

Lines the rep says, one at a time. Instruction lives in a label, not inside the line.

04

The methodology was a sticker, not a structure

Blocks carried a technique tag. The text underneath was the same for any technique.

Before · real output

"PROBLEM · CHALLENGER" over a paragraph that could have carried any label. The technique never changed the shape of the conversation.

Deal qualification (MEDDPICC) and conversation methodology were also mixed into one field, answering two different questions at once.

Now · challenger arc [SAMPLE]
1Context2Insight / reframe3Business impact4New way5Solution relevance6Commitment

SAMPLEEvery block states which step it serves. SPIN and NEPQ each run their own sequence. MEDDPICC sits below as a separate evidence checklist.

The technique is chosen first. Everything else is written inside it.

05

Numbers came from nowhere

Percentages, headcounts and confidence scores arrived with no source behind them.

Before · real output

"What if we could show you how to reduce the time spent on manual prep by 50% and increase your partnership close rates by 15%?"

"A focused group of 10 to 15 sellers" — proposed to a founder who works alone. The same line shipped to two different buyers, word for word.

Confidence "85" also shipped as a bare number with nothing behind it.

Now · [SAMPLE]

BASEShe works alone. Any pilot must fit one person. Source: her own profile.

Every number needs a receipt or the sentence does not exist. No receipts, no numbers.

06

One script fit all buyers

Different people, different reads, different techniques. Same words.

Before · real output

18 lines identical between two buyers running different techniques. Nine full spoken sentences, word for word, despite different personality reads.

The generator filled a template. The buyer only changed the nouns.

Now

MEASUREDLine overlap between buyers is counted. Repeated lines are treated as defects, not style. Target: zero identical spoken sentences.

Same company, two buyers, two different calls. The proof sits below.

07

It read like a build log

Fourteen pages. Endpoints, seconds, byte counts, raw links.

Before · real output

"POST /api/parse · 2.9 s · 12,285 bytes" opened the document. The buyer appeared three pages later. Eight source links, mixed date formats, the same facts repeated three times.

A rep had 90 seconds before a call. The document needed an hour.

Now

BASEOne page. Who they are, how they decide, the arc, the first line, what is still unknown. Nothing else.

Printable per buyer. What the rep reads is what the coach tracks.

08

The rep never had their own CRM seat

Only the manager connected a CRM. The rep inherited the whole account.

Before · real state

"Only your workspace owner or manager can connect to data sources." A rep accepting an invite landed in a workspace already tied to one shared connection, pulling ten thousand leads that were not theirs.

No step in the invite flow asked the rep to connect their own seat.

Now · shipped

SHIPPEDEach rep connects their own seat during invite setup. The picker shows the team's CRM first. Only their leads come in.

The data foundation starts correct: one seat, one rep, their own leads.

✦

Same three buyers, three different calls

Imported from CRM. Same company for two of them. The calls do not overlap.
