Pre-call, rebuilt

It used to invent the buyer. Now it runs the call.

Three failures broke the old pre-call, and each one is now closed. It invented the person. It narrated the call instead of running it. And it buried both problems under fourteen pages. The evidence for every claim is below, in the product's own words.

Picture the rep with ninety seconds before the call, opening a brief about a different person with the same name. That is where this started.

1Rep connects their own CRM seat→ 2The right person is found→ 3Personality read, as hypothesis→ 4The method shapes the script→ 5Coach speaks only on a missed step
Act one · it invented the buyer

Every line the rep read inherited one original sin: the person underneath it was wrong, or made up.

01

It researched the wrong person

Search went by name alone. Same name, different person, wrong career.

Before · real output

"Group Chief Revenue Officer & Head of Partnerships" — a title that belonged to someone else with the same name. The whole brief described the wrong career.

Tracing each wrong fact later took a full audit of a recorded call.

Now

BASEThe CRM fields drive every search: name, company and title together. A bare name never triggers research.

Why it holds: wrong-person is designed out at the input, not caught at the output.

02

The main question was invented

A generated "best question to ask" sat on every opportunity, built on nothing.

Before · real output

"How do you currently ensure sales teams is interpreting her strategy and operations…"

Asked about a role the buyer never held, on the wrong person's data.

No source. No reason for that question to exist. No reason for it to be first.

Now

SHIPPEDThe generated question is gone. One meeting objective stays, written per call from the record.

Why it holds: anything on the card needs a reason to exist, and the reason is checkable.

03

Numbers came from nowhere

Percentages, headcounts and confidence scores arrived with nothing behind them.

Before · real output

"…reduce the time spent on manual prep by 50% and increase your partnership close rates by 15%?"

"A focused group of 10 to 15 sellers" — proposed to a founder who works alone. The same line shipped to two buyers, word for word.

Confidence "85" also shipped as a bare number. Nothing behind it.

Now · [SAMPLE]

BASEShe works alone. Any pilot must fit one person. Source: her own profile.

Why it holds: every number needs a receipt, or the sentence is not written.

Act two · it narrated the call instead of running it

The technique was a sticker. The script was stage directions. Two different buyers got the same words.

04

One script fit all buyers

Different people, different reads, different techniques. Same words.

Before · real output

18 lines identical between two buyers running different techniques. Nine full spoken sentences, word for word.

The generator filled a template. Only the nouns changed.

Now

COUNTEDLine overlap between buyers is measured on every generation. Repeated lines are defects. Target: zero.

Why it holds: the failure that hid in prose is now a number that gates shipping.

05

A document about the call, not a script

The "script" narrated the call. The rep needed lines to say.

Before · real output

"When she answers what made the call worth taking, name it back once and hold it for the close."

Stage directions, twenty-eight in a list. Nothing a rep could say out loud.

Now · [SAMPLE]

Ask: "What made this call worth taking for you?"

Then wait. Let the buyer speak first.

Why it holds: lines the rep says, one at a time. Instruction lives in the label, not inside the line.

06

The methodology was a sticker, not a structure

Blocks carried a technique tag. The text was the same under any tag.

Before · real output

"PROBLEM · CHALLENGER" over a paragraph that could carry any label. The technique never changed the shape of the conversation.

Deal qualification and conversation method also shared one field, answering two questions at once.

Now · challenger arc [SAMPLE]
1Context2Insight / reframe3Business impact4New way5Solution relevance6Commitment

SAMPLEEvery block states which step it serves. SPIN and NEPQ run their own sequences. Qualification sits below, separate.

Why it holds: the technique is chosen first. Everything else is written inside it.

Act three · wrong shape, wrong foundation

Even correct intelligence arrived in a form nobody could use, fed by a seat that was never the rep's.

07

It read like a build log

Fourteen pages. Endpoints, seconds, byte counts, raw links.

Before · real output

"POST /api/parse · 2.9 s · 12,285 bytes" opened the document. The buyer appeared three pages later. Eight raw links. The same facts three times.

A rep had ninety seconds. The document needed an hour.

Now

BASEOne page. Who they are, how they decide, the arc, the first line, what is still unknown. Fourteen pages became one screen.

Why it holds: the page is printable per buyer, and it is the same plan the coach tracks live.

08

The rep never had their own CRM seat

Only the manager connected a CRM. The rep inherited everything.

Before · real state

"Only your workspace owner or manager can connect to data sources." A rep accepting an invite inherited one shared connection and ten thousand leads that were not theirs.

No step in the invite flow ever asked the rep to connect their own seat.

Now · shipped

SHIPPEDEach rep connects their own seat during invite setup. The picker leads with the team's CRM. Only their leads come in.

Why it holds: the data foundation starts correct. One seat, one rep, their own leads.

The eight fixes, one line each

  1. Wrong person: CRM fields drive the search. A name alone triggers nothing.
  2. Invented question: removed. One sourced objective per call.
  3. Invented numbers: no receipt, no sentence.
  4. One script for all: repeated lines counted as defects. Target zero.
  5. Narration: lines the rep says, one at a time.
  6. Sticker technique: the method is chosen first and every block serves a step of it.
  7. Build log: fourteen pages became one screen.
  8. No seat: every rep connects their own, filtered to the team's CRM.

The standard we hold ourselves to · targets, not shipped
  • Blueprint quality scored by a judge that reads. Floor: 88 of 100 per buyer, every note with a quoted receipt.
  • Zero identical spoken lines between buyers, counted on every generation.
  • The coach tracks the same arc the rep prepared. On track means silence. A missed step means one prompt.
  • One printable page per buyer, generated from the same plan the coach runs.

The guess is gone. What replaces it has to prove itself, every generation, against numbers.
