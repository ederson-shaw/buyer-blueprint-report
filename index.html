04

Removed on purpose

Wrong data did not get rewritten. It got removed, with the rule that killed it.

  • The ask field

    How do you currently ensure sales teams is interpreting her strategy and operations…

    A generated "best question" built on nothing. Replaced by one meeting objective, per call, from the record.

  • Headcount fiction

    A focused group of 10 to 15 sellers, proposed to a founder who works alone.

    Rule: team size comes from the record or stays an open question.

  • Invented numbers

    Reduce the time spent on manual prep by 50% and increase your partnership close rates by 15%?

    Rule: no receipt, no sentence. The new script says: "I would not give you an invented accuracy percentage."

  • The template read

    Near-identical DISC numbers for different humans, months apart.

    Rule: the read discriminates or it does not ship. Each buyer gets a read from their own profile.

  • The build log

    POST /api/parse · 2.9 s · 12,285 bytes opened the old document.

    Fourteen pages. Replaced by the script the rep reads: first move, stages, unknowns.

05

The standard

Format is live. Quality is still judged.
  • The morning generation scored 32, 38 and 36 of 100 under a judge that reads. The evening record fixes the named defects: fabrication, example leak, shared lines.
  • Identical lines between buyers, measured after the fix: zero, in all three pairs.
  • The judge re-runs on this record. The floor is 88 of 100 per buyer, receipt quoted per note.
  • Nothing on this page claims a score. Fields are shown as they are, from the records.

The data was always there. The bar it has to clear now is written down.
